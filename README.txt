Control your Mitsubishi Electric air conditioners and heat pumps directly over your local network — no cloud, no MELCloud account required.

This app talks straight to the Mitsubishi Wi-Fi interface adapter (MAC-558IF-E, MAC-567IF-E, MAC-568IF-E, MAC-578IF-E, MAC-588IF-E) fitted to your indoor unit, using the open ECHONET Lite protocol.

Features:
- Power on/off
- Operation mode: Automatic, Cooling, Heating, Dehumidify and Fan only
- Target temperature (16–31 °C)
- Room and outdoor temperature readings
- Fan speed: Auto, Quiet, Low, Medium, High and Maximum
- Vertical vane control: Auto, Top, Upper, Middle, Lower, Bottom and Swing
- All capabilities available in Flows as triggers, conditions and actions

Units are discovered automatically on your network, or can be added by IP address. Changes made with the IR remote or the MELCloud app are picked up on a configurable poll interval, so Homey always shows the current state.

Everything runs locally on your Homey — fast, private and independent of any cloud service.
